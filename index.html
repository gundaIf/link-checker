✴︎ How it works

We download the page, read every <a href>, resolve relative links, and sort them into rooms. “Check where links go” follows each link’s redirects and reports the final address and status.

✴︎ Privacy

No accounts and no tracking. The URL you enter is fetched by our server and not stored; the extracted results stay in your browser.
