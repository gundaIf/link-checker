How it works

We download the page, read every <a href>, resolve relative links, and group them. “Check where links go” follows each link’s redirects and reports the final address and status.

Privacy

No accounts and no tracking. The URL you enter is fetched by our server and not stored; the extracted results stay in your browser.

Types

Internal links stay on the same site (www. is ignored). Social, files and media, email and phone, and same-page anchors are split out so the external list stays clean.
